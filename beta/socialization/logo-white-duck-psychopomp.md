# Logo direction — the white duck (psychopomp / open-protocols ferry)

date: 2026-07-23
status: **working candidate (brand tier). Brief and direction only — no rendered asset.** One of the two
candidates registered at `../OPEN-THREADS.md` T61. Primacy unsettled: the current marks are the drystone
badge (`logo-drystone-stacking.md`) and the CROFT.ing wall-to-gate wordmark (`logo-croft-ing-wall-to-gate.md`);
whether Croft wants an animal/ferry mark at all is an open call (T4/T61).

**The idea.** In the Gilded Weaver fable (`the-gilded-weaver-fable.md`) the white duck is the **psychopomp** —
the helper that ferries people across the water out of the enclosure to the open shore. Mapped to Croft, the
duck = **open protocols / interoperability / the exit**: safe passage across a hostile digital divide. A mark
in this direction would carry that meaning without a word.

**Hard constraint: distinct from DuckDuckGo.** DDG relies on a friendly cartoon mallard (orange bill,
fedora/bowtie). This direction goes the opposite way — mythic, editorial, minimal:

- **Ditch the mascot for minimalism.** Single-line silhouette, negative space, architectural/editorial — not
  cartoon illustration.
- **Stark palette, not warm.** Pure white on deep charcoal / obsidian / midnight-blue (the dark forest, the
  threshold of water) — no oranges/yellows/greens.
- **Mythic/structural motif.** Hint at a guide, a bridge, a ferry, a clean vector wave — safe passage, not a
  static animal icon.

Three concepts to compare when an asset is commissioned:

1. **Negative-space ripple (the ferry motif).** A minimalist white duck profile cutting horizontally, a
   single geometric arc beneath as the water line. Modern, clean; emphasizes movement/guidance.
2. **Geometric origami wing (the open-source vibe).** A faceted/origami white duck wing whose feather lines
   double as pathways/circuit lines. Technical, precise, structural.
3. **Monoline silhouette (the editorial mark).** A continuous single-line white stroke of a gliding duck on
   dark. Sophisticated, literary — reads like an independent press or a protocol logo, not a startup mascot.

**Fit with the existing marks.** The drystone/croft-ing marks say *craft, land, dry-stacked commons*; a duck
says *exit, ferry, open water*. They are not obviously one system — hence the primacy/should-we-have-an-
animal-mark question is a real open call, not a foregone adoption.

**Open calls (the user's, T61).** Adopt a ferry/animal mark at all? If so, which of the three concepts, and
does it sit as a secondary mark (e.g. for the "leave the walled garden" narrative) beside the primary
drystone/wordmark, or compete for primacy?
