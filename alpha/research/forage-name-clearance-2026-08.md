## The name

**Forage**, at **forage.fyi** (`/f/<community>` paths; members = "foragers"). The register
case: it is graze's sibling verb — the loved-but-taken first choice — a common pastoral
word with the "nature behavior everyone instantly understands" property (the tweet test),
and `.fyi` supplies the news/information tilt by itself. Sound-alike filter (the session's
standing test: wimp/ed/leet/Brooke all killed earlier candidates): clean — nearest
neighbors "porridge" (distant) and no person-name adjacency.
