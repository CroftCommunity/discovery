## TL;DR

- **"Amble" is a crowded, medium-to-high-risk name.** The most acute conflict is a live app literally
  called **"amble — social media without influence"** run by **AMBLE NZ LIMITED** at **amble.social** —
  a direct-category competitor already occupying the exact word and a key domain. There is no live US
  federal AMBLE registration in the core social/telecom classes (38/45), but two pending Class 9
  software applications exist.
- **The trademark register is passable but not clean:** the two historically registered AMBLE marks
  nearest software (AMBLE GENERATION, e-cigarettes; AMBLE LLC, advertising) are both **DEAD**, but a
  pending Class 9 filing for language/cultural *reading* software (Serial 99174346) sits squarely in
  the product's lane.
- **MEANDER (US Serial 77642304 / Reg. 3738941) is DEAD** — Cancelled under Section 8 as of 2016-08-26
  — so on pure clearance grounds Meander's US register is now *cleaner* in Class 42 than Amble's is in
  Class 9. This weighs in the runner-up comparison.

## Key Findings

1. **No live US registration in classes 38/41/45**; the software-class picture is two *pending* (not
   yet examined) Class 9 applications.
2. **The marketplace is heavily crowded** — a dozen-plus distinct "Amble/Ambl/Ambulo/AmbleVerse"
   products across fitness, walking-safety, paratransit, language learning, restaurant booking, and —
   critically — social media.
3. **The single most dangerous conflict is AMBLE NZ LIMITED's "amble" social app** — same word,
   overlapping "mindful social sharing" category, and it holds amble.social.
4. **The domain landscape is tight:** amble.com is long-held and not for sale; amble.social is occupied
   by the competitor; amble.io and amblex.com are brokered for sale; the npm package name "amble" is
   taken.
5. **The atproto/Bluesky ecosystem niche is clear** — no client, feed, PDS host, or labeler named Amble
   was found — a meaningful positive.
6. **The MEANDER word mark is dead**, resolving the open Meander clearance item.

### E. The word "Ambler(s)" and geographic marks

- **Eric Ambler** (1909–1998), the celebrated British spy/thriller novelist, is a strong literary
  association in exactly the reading/books space — relevant to brand connotation and demonym color, but
  **not a trademark blocker**.
- **Amble, Northumberland** — coastal town (parish pop. 5,860, 2021 Census) — and **Ambleside** (Lake
  District) are geographic place names. Geographic terms can raise "primarily geographically
  descriptive" objections, but the product does not originate there and "amble" is an ordinary English
  verb; its **primary significance is the dictionary meaning**, so geographic-descriptiveness risk is
  low.
- The dictionary meaning makes **"AMBLE" suggestive/arguably weak** — easier to coexist/register
  alongside others, but **harder to enforce broadly** against the many existing users.
- **"Ambler(s)" as a demonym is workable** and not obviously blocked — though the women's-safety Amble
  already calls its users "Amblers."

### G. MEANDER comparative (runner-up name)

- **MEANDER — Archiform Ltd**, US Serial 77642304, Reg. 3738941, Class 42 (computer services;
  virtual-tour indexing for real estate). Filed 2008-12-31, registered 2010-01-19. Status: **"710 –
  Cancelled – Section 8,"** status date **2016-08-26**. **DEAD.** The open Meander item is resolved.
- Other MEANDER marks exist (Meander Travel Ltd, UK, classes 39/41; a wine mark; a Chinese bicycle-parts
  mark) but **none is a live, blocking mark in the software/social core** the way Amble's live Class 9
  language-software application (99174346) is.
- **Clearance bottom line:** on pure register cleanliness in the relevant software/services classes,
  **Meander is currently the *safer* mark** — its nearest computer-services registration is dead, and no
  live software/social MEANDER conflict was identified, whereas Amble has (a) a live direct social-app
  common-law user, (b) a pending Class 9 reading/education software application, and (c) an occupied
  .social domain. Amble's advantages are the open atproto niche and (if affordable) amble.ing — marketing
  strengths, not clearance strengths.

## Recommendations

**Stage 1 — before any public commitment (now):**
1. Order a full US + UK + EU knockout search from trademark counsel focused on classes 9, 38, 41, 42, 45.
   Instruct them to (a) pull TSDR status for Serials **99174346** (language/culture software) and
   **99446780** (telemedicine apps); (b) assess **AMBLE NZ** and **Ambl Group (UK)** as potential
   common-law senior users; (c) check UK/EU registers on TMview/One-IPO.
2. Get a live registrar quote on **amble.ing** to confirm whether it is premium-tier and at what
   recurring cost; price defensive fallbacks (getamble.*, tryamble.*, amble.blue, amble.ink) in
   parallel.
3. Confirm handle availability directly: bsky.app/profile/amble.bsky.social, github.com/amble, and the
   X/Instagram/YouTube handles.

**Stage 2 — go / no-go thresholds:**
- **Proceed with "Amble"** only if ALL hold: (a) counsel confirms no live, enforceable US/UK/EU
  registration in 9/38/41/42/45 that a social reading app would infringe; (b) the AMBLE NZ social app is
  judged a low common-law threat (small scale — ~100+ downloads, NZ-based — and coexistence plausible);
  (c) amble.ing is obtainable at a non-premium or acceptable price.
- **Reconsider / pivot to Meander** if ANY hold: the AMBLE NZ app shows meaningful US traction or files a
  US application; the Class 9 language-software application (99174346) matures toward registration with a
  broad reading/education recitation; or amble.ing prices as a five-figure-plus premium.

**Stage 3 — on adoption:**
- File your own US intent-to-use applications promptly to establish priority: Class 9 (downloadable
  social-networking/reading software), Class 42 (SaaS), and Class 45 (online social networking services).
- Adopt a distinctive logo/wordmark treatment and the two-word lockup (**"Amble by Croft"**) to
  strengthen an otherwise-suggestive mark and improve enforceability.
- Use a **custom-domain Bluesky handle (amble.ing)** to sidestep the bsky.social namespace and reinforce
  brand identity on atproto.
- **Send the AMBLE NZ coexistence letter before launch** and pursue the answer — silence is not consent,
  and unwritten coexistence ripens reverse-confusion risk exactly when the product succeeds. (This is the
  load-bearing mitigation; see the go/no-go dialogue.)
