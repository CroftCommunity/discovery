EMOJI WARS — HOW IT WORKS

Two modes (⚙). Sling: fling your hero at the villains; break every target-material emoji to clear. Drop: the hero spawns at the start pad and rolls with gravity; tap to hop while touching a surface; reach the 🏁 goal without touching a villain. Tall levels (⚙ level shape) suit drop mode.
Placing. Arm a shape in the tray, tap the board to stamp it in the current material. 🖌 paint is different: drag to color in a freeform blob.
Adjusting (✋). Tap a piece to select. Drag to move — it lifts above your finger and edges magnet-snap flush (blobs skip the magnet). Pinch resizes. Twist or ⟳ rotates. The arrow pad nudges one grid square per tap and appears away from the piece.
Looking (🔍). Toggle 🔍, then pinch to zoom up to 4×, drag to pan, double-tap to reset.
Materials. wood breaks, stone barely breaks, metal never breaks, ice is slippery and melts during Test, rubber bounces, target marks villains.
Behaviors. ⚓ anchors. ↔ patrol path. 🏁 marks the goal zone for drop mode. ✎ attaches a note — your intent in words, riding in the schema.
Backdrops. Pick scenery, or 🖼 upload your own image. 📋 copies a brief for an image agent sized to this level's shape.
Test (▶). Gravity exists only here. ■ returns to editing, level untouched.
The schema ({ }). The level is JSON. Copy it into a Claude chat; paste Claude's levels back and Load. 💾 saves named versions grouped into scenes.
